#### Chapter Layout (11 chapters, ~44–46 pages total)

Each chapter follows a consistent layout:

**Chapter Start Pages (11 pages):**
- Full chapter title at top: "Chapter X: [Title]"
- Small icon/spot illustration at top or bottom
- Chapter number in large numeral

**Text + Illustration Pages:**
- Each chapter has ~2 pages of pure text plus 2–3 illustration placements
- Layout alternates between:
  - **Full-page text** (no illustration) — most of the text content
  - **Half-page illustration + text** — illustration at top (3" tall), text below
  - **Full-page illustration** — reserved for dramatic moments (cliff scenes, flash flood, etc.)

## Cover Concept: "Magic Tree House" Style

### Front Cover

- **Background:** Dramatic sunset over a painted canyon — deep oranges, purples, and golds
- **Foreground:** Silhouette of Outlaw (dark stallion) standing on a rocky ridge, mane blowing in the wind
- **Title:** "OUTLAW" in large bold Western-style font (think adventure lettering), white with drop shadow
- **Subtitle:** "The Great Canyon Crossing" in smaller elegant font below
- **Tagline:** (optional, bottom) "A wild horse adventure"
- **Author line:** "StorySprout Press" at very bottom
- **Age bracket note:** Small "Ages 6–9" badge in corner

### Back Cover

- **Background:** Continuation of canyon sunset (wraparound or complementary color)
- **Blurb:** 3–4 sentence hook:
  > "The first frost is here, and Outlaw must lead his herd through the treacherous Painted Canyon before the blizzard hits. But with a rival stallion challenging his leadership and danger lurking around every bend, can Outlaw bring them all to safety before winter closes in?"
- **Barcode area:** Standard KDP placement (bottom right)

### Spine

- Book title: "OUTLAW" (vertical, readable top-to-bottom)
- Author: "StorySprout Press"
- Spine width at ~55 pages on 6"×9" = ~0.3" (thin but printable)

## Build Approach (Future)

When ready to build the PDF, use ReportLab (as with guidebook/journal):

**Script:** `make_outlaw_book1.py`
**Tools:** Python + ReportLab + Pillow
**Process:**
1. Load each chapter text from the .md file
2. Parse out illustration markers
3. Convert each PNG to grayscale, resize to fit 6"×9" pages
4. Build page by page: chapter headers, body text, illustration blocks
5. Generate full print-ready PDF with cover

## Key Notes for Build Time

1. **All 30 illustrations are done** — no need to wait for ch08–11. Full asset set available.
2. **Illustrations are 1024×1024 square** — for 6"×9" portrait pages, they'll need to be placed at top or middle with text below (or scaled to fit full page with cropping to 6"×4.5" for dramatic scenes)
3. **Grayscale conversion** — the PNGs are full RGB color; use Pillow's `Image.convert('L')` for print-ready grayscale
4. **Chapter breaks** — each chapter should start on a new page (right-hand if possible)
5. **Kid-friendly font** — use 13pt serif (Times/Garamond) for body, bold 20pt for chapter titles
